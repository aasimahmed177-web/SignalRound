Round log

Outreach drafts: ours won blind in round 2 (after losing 4). Each draft quotes the funding article or the job listing, uses the case study that fits the buyer, and ends with the required call ask. Strict filters mean only 15 drafts from 416 leads; the rest were skipped for weak fit, a bad inbox or a wrong website.
Funded + hiring: loop stopped after 5 losses. Startups funded this week rarely post fresh marketing roles yet. 10 of 11 roles found predate the round, and the one "new" role was a repost. Every email checked was verbatim (0 made up). Final fixes: detect reposts, website must match the funding article, CEO emails printed on contact pages, and 7 held-back review bugs.
Extra uses. Industry tag won in round 1 (gap: 3 companies labelled from the wrong website). Ranked shortlist won in round 2 after location became a hard filter; LinkedIn's apply buttons all require sign-in. Reddit posts won with only 1 row, because Reddit blocks most logged-out search. Funded-and-hiring lost twice to YC's marketing jobs (too few marketing roles, no founder emails) and is rebuilding.
Posted emails only. Contacts are saved only with an email posted on a public page (committed e574a8f). Founders or companies without one are dropped, and no verifier is used. Fable 5.1 picked 4 more uses, now building.
Piece 3, round 1: ours won blind. 75 startups funded pre-seed to Series C across countries. 65 have founder names and 36 have a usable public inbox. The critic checked 18 emails on their source pages: all 18 were there verbatim, and none were guessed. Entrackr had 28 rounds with no founders, emails or websites. Gaps: 4 of 13 sampled founders were the wrong person (an investor or customer exec named in the article), and 1 website was wrong. Only 1 personal founder email was found.

The critic checked 14 sources: 14/14 emails verbatim, and 0 of the links it checked were dead. hiring.cafe had 2,663 jobs and 0 emails. Gaps: about 35 emails are help or accessibility inboxes, not CV inboxes. Most apply links point to Indeed/LinkedIn rather than the employer.
